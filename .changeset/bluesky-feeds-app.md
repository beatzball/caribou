Bluesky: pinned feeds on Home. With a Bluesky account active, Home shows tabs: Following first, then your pinned feeds. Each tab is its own address, so Back keeps your place in it. A new Feeds item lists your saved feeds and opens one as a Home tab. A feed that is gone or does not load says so in its tab. Caribou only reads your feeds; it does not change them. Mastodon Home does not change. A feed's posts hide when you mute or block their author, as on Following. Retry on the Feeds page keeps focus.
