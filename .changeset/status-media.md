Posts now show their media under the text: images, video, GIFs (gifv) and audio. One item is full width; two to four share a grid. Each box keeps its size before the file arrives, so the list does not jump. A click on an image opens it large in a lightbox (Esc or Close shuts it, and focus goes back to the image). Media with a description has an ALT button that shows the text. A GIF plays muted on a loop, and waits with controls when the reader asks for reduced motion. Media on a sensitive post stays hidden until the reader clicks "Show sensitive media". Boosts show the boosted post's media, and the server render of `/local` and `/public` includes the media.
