Polls. A post shows its poll: options, bars, shares, voters, the end time, the reader's choices, and "Ended". The server renders the results. A signed-in reader on the post's server votes with radio buttons or checkboxes, and can refresh the poll. The compose dialog has a poll builder: options up to the server's limit, a duration, several choices, and hidden totals. A poll and media exclude each other.
