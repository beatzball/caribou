`fetchNotifications` reads `listNotifications`: like, repost, follow, mention, reply and quote map to Caribou types; every other reason, and a notification whose post is gone, is skipped and never throws. Posts come from `getPosts` with provenance and moderation. A page carries the AppView cursor and a seen hint. `sinceId` pages from the top until it meets that notification. New `getUnreadCount` and `updateSeen`. Reasons are looked up in a `Map`, so names like `constructor` are skipped. Catch-up also stops at the first notification older than `sinceAt`, so a deleted anchor is not read past. The seen hint also names the raw top's URI (`newestUri`). Notification authors and posts carry the labeler coverage their own answer named (`atproto-content-labelers`), so the reader veil can settle for them. The signed-in moderation source is now `readerModerationSource` (in `./moderation`), and `BlueskyModerationSource` takes an optional `coverage`.
