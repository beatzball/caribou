A `/notifications` page lists mentions, favourites, boosts, follows, follow requests, ended polls and edits, with an All / Mentions filter. A type the app does not know shows a plain line instead of breaking the list. The list checks for new notifications every 60 seconds while the tab is visible. The nav rail has a Notifications item after Local, also in the phone bar, with an unread count, which clears when the page is shown; screen readers hear "Notifications, 3 unread".
