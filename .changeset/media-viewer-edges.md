On a phone, the media viewer's dim now reaches the top and bottom of the screen. While the viewer is open, the browser's own bars take the same dim: Safari 26 on iOS from a thin strip at each edge of the page, older Safari and Chrome from the theme color. Closing the viewer gives the bars the page color back, also after a theme change while it was open. Close, the key hint and the description stay clear of the notch and the home indicator, in portrait and landscape.
