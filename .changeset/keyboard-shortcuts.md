Add keyboard shortcuts. `j` and `k` move focus to the next and previous post, in every list of posts. `o` or Enter opens the post. `f`, `b` and `r` favourite, boost and reply to it. `n` writes a new post, `/` focuses the search box, and `g` then `h`, `n`, `l` or `s` goes to Home, Notifications, Lists or Search. `?` shows the list of shortcuts. No shortcut fires while you type, while a dialog is open, or with Ctrl, Meta or Alt. A new Keyboard section in Settings turns them off. The right rail's Keyboard shortcuts item now links there.
