Add search. `/search?q=` has People, Hashtags and Posts tabs, and the address follows the box as you type. A search box sits in the right rail, and Search is in the nav rail (in More on a phone). Pasting a post or profile address opens it in the app. Signed out, search asks the server the public timelines use, which finds people and hashtags but not posts.
