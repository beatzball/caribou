Add lists. `/lists` shows your lists, with New list, Edit (title, who to show replies to, and on Mastodon 4.2+ whether to hide the posts from Home) and Delete, which asks first. `/lists/<id>` shows the list's timeline, with its title, Edit list and a Members panel where you can remove an account. A profile's menu has "Add to lists". Lists is in the nav for a signed-in reader.
