Zoom the image in the media viewer, not the page. The viewer now fills most of the screen. Pinch, double-tap, ctrl+wheel or a trackpad pinch zoom and pan only the image, and a zoomed image uses the whole viewer. The `+`, `-` and `0` keys zoom, and the arrow keys move a zoomed image; the dialog names these keys. The Close bar stays in place, and closing resets the zoom. Page zoom stays on everywhere else.
