Bluesky muted words on the filters screen. With a Bluesky account, /settings/moderation lists the muted words and adds or removes them: the word, text and tags or tags only, how long, and everyone or only people you do not follow. An expired word stays in the list. After a save, the words apply with no page reload. The Mastodon filters screen is as before.
