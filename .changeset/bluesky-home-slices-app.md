Bluesky Home shows threads as bsky.app does. A reply is drawn with its parent above it, and the root when it differs, joined by a thread line. A "…" marker shows when posts are skipped. Each post keeps its own card, moderation and time link. Each thread shows once, at its newest entry, across pages. Reposts stay on their own. Mastodon is unchanged.
