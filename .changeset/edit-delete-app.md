Edit and delete your own posts. The post menu on your own posts has Edit, Delete and Delete & redraft. Edit opens the compose dialog with the post's text as the server has it now; visibility and media stay as they are. A post with a poll cannot be edited, because Mastodon would remove the poll and its votes. Delete asks first, and focus moves to the next post. Delete & redraft opens a new post with what the deleted one had, saved as a draft at once; it never replaces another unsent draft, and opens first the next time you write a new post. An edited post shows "edited" next to its time; it opens the edit history, newest first.
