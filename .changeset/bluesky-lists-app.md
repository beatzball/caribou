Lists work for a Bluesky account. The nav rail shows Lists. The New list and Edit list dialogs ask for the title only and say that Bluesky lists are public. A title over 64 bytes is refused. The list picker does not ask for a follow. Member rows go through moderation, and a member with no decision is covered. A Bluesky member links to its handle or DID. Mastodon lists do not change.
