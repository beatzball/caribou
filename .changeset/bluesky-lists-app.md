Lists work for a Bluesky account. The nav rail shows Lists. The New list and Edit list dialogs ask for the title only and say that Bluesky lists are public. A title over 64 bytes is refused. The list picker does not ask for a follow. Member rows go through moderation, and a member with no decision is covered. A Bluesky member links to its handle or DID. Mastodon lists do not change. Member rows follow the reader veil. The Bluesky delete confirm does not say the accounts stay followed, and the signed-out list placeholders name no network. An open Members panel loads again when its cache is dropped.
