Follow and unfollow from a profile. The profile header shows Follow, Following (Unfollow under the pointer or focus) or Requested, and "Follows you". The button appears after mount for a signed-in reader and never on the reader's own profile. On a profile from another instance the header first looks the account up on the reader's server; when that server does not know it, there is no button.
