Going back to Home, Local, Public, a profile or a thread is instant. The page shows what it showed last time at once, then checks for changes in the background. New posts go to the "N new posts" banner, so the list does not move; new replies in a thread appear at the end, and the next visit keeps that order; edits and counts update in place. Back lands where you were. A page older than five minutes says "Updating…" while it refreshes. A failed refresh or page load keeps the list on screen with a Retry button, and does not retry by itself. The cache is cleared when the active account changes.
