`<caribou-button>` takes `pressed`, `label` and `hint`. `pressed` makes a text button a toggle, with the same three states as on `<caribou-icon-button>`. `label` and `hint` name the inner button and give it a tooltip. A post's "edited" mark and the media Show sensitive media, Hide and lightbox Close buttons now use `<caribou-button>`.
