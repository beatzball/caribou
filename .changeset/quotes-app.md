Show quote posts (Mastodon 4.5). A quoted post shows as a small card inside the post that quotes it: the author, the text, a media summary and a link to its thread. A quote that shows no post (pending, declined, removed, deleted, not allowed, or by an account the reader blocked or muted) shows a short note. The quoted post's content warning, the reader's filters, mutes and blocks, and the reader's edits and deletes all apply. A quote inside a quoted post is a link, not another card. The server renders the quote, so it works without JavaScript.
